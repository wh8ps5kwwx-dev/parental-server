Terms of Use

Last updated: July 2026

These terms cover use of the MYRana graduation project software and this landing site. Product names: parent حماية الأطفال, child أكاديمية العباقرة.

1. Educational purpose

MYRana is a university graduation / portfolio demonstration of a stealthy cybersecurity-based parental monitoring system. It is provided for education, evaluation, and demonstration — not as a commercial consumer service with uptime guarantees.

2. Intended use

  • Guardians may install the parent experience and link child devices they are responsible for.
  • Child devices should be configured with required Android permissions by the guardian.
  • Use is limited to lawful parental supervision within a family / caregiving context.

3. What the software does

Subject to platform limits and correct permissions, MYRana may:

  • Link parent and child via Gmail OTP and CHILD device codes.
  • Collect and display usage statistics and screen-time related data.
  • Block or freeze apps and filter sites/content via Accessibility on Android.
  • Deliver alerts, reports, and guardian messages through the server.

MYRana does not provide GPS location tracking in the implemented applications. Do not rely on this project for locating a missing child or for geofencing.

4. What the software does not guarantee

  • Complete prevention of all harmful content or bypass attempts.
  • Parity with commercial products (e.g. continuous push, encrypted-at-rest DB, Play Store distribution).
  • Full enforcement on iOS — Android is the primary monitored platform.
  • Permanent storage on free Render instances without durable disk configuration.

5. Guardian responsibilities

  • Obtain appropriate consent within the family before monitoring a child’s device.
  • Keep account credentials and OTP email access secure.
  • Understand that Accessibility and Usage Access are powerful permissions and should be granted only for this family-control purpose.
  • Use blocks, filters, and messages in a way that remains protective rather than abusive.

6. Acceptable use

You may not use MYRana to:

  • Monitor adults or devices without lawful authority and consent.
  • Stalk, harass, or unlawfully surveil any person.
  • Attack, probe, or disrupt the Render API beyond normal app traffic.
  • Misrepresent the project as providing location tracking or other unimplemented capabilities.
